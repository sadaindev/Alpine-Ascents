Techniques & Equipment

Build knowledge with qualified instruction and careful practice.

Core skills

  • Navigation: read maps, understand route descriptions and carry backup navigation.
  • Weather awareness:check forecasts and recognise when conditions are deteriorating.
  • Movement:learn efficient walking, scrambling and terrain-specific movement from trained instructors.
  • Team communication:agree on plans, check in regularly and speak up about concerns.
  • Emergency planning: know local emergency procedures and carry suitable communication equipment.



Equipment checklist

Clothing

Layered clothing, waterproof outerwear, warm hat and gloves suited to conditions.

Essentials

Navigation, headlamp, first-aid kit, sun protection, food and enough water.

Technical gear

Helmets, ropes, harnesses, footwear and other equipment selected for the route and used with proper training.
