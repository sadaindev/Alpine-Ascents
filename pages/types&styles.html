Types & Styles of
Mountaineering

Different environments call for different skills and preparation.



Trekking

Walking on established mountain trails, often over multiple days. Fitness, navigation, weather awareness and suitable footwear matter.



Alpine Mountaineering

Combines hiking, scrambling and sometimes snow or rock travel. Conditions can change quickly, so planning is essential.



High-Altitude Climbing

Expeditions at high elevations require acclimatisation, medical awareness, experienced leadership and flexible schedules.



Rock Climbing

Focuses on moving over rock using climbing shoes, ropes and protection where appropriate. Learn with qualified instruction.



Snow & Ice Climbing

Involves specialised equipment and training for frozen terrain. Ice and avalanche conditions must be assessed by trained people.



Expedition Style

Longer journeys may use camps, support teams and staged acclimatisation to manage logistics in remote areas
