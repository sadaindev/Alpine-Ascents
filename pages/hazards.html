Hazards & Risk Awareness

Recognising changing conditions helps teams make safer decisions.

Weather


Rapid Weather Changes

Wind, rain, snow, lightning and low visibility can make a route unsafe. Check forecasts and be ready to turn back.

Terrain


Falls & Loose Rock

Uneven ground, exposed edges and falling debris are serious risks. Choose routes that match the team’s training.

Snow


Avalanche Conditions

Snowpack can be unstable. Avalanche terrain requires specialist education, local forecasts and appropriate equipment.

Altitude


Altitude Illness

High altitude can affect health. Ascend gradually, learn warning signs and seek qualified medical advice; serious symptoms require prompt descent and emergency help.

Cold


Cold & Dehydration

Plan clothing, hydration, food and rest. Monitor the group and respond early when someone is struggling.

Planning


Isolation & Delays

Share an itinerary, set a turnaround time, carry emergency supplies and know how to contact local rescue services.

Golden rule: Reaching the summit is optional; returning safely is essential. If conditions or the team’s ability change, turning back is a responsible decision.
