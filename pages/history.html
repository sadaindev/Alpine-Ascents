The History of Mountaineering

From early mountain journeys to modern expeditions.

Origins & evolution

From exploration to a global pursuit

People have crossed and lived among mountains for centuries. Mountaineering as a recognised sporting activity grew as explorers began attempting summits for discovery, science and personal challenge.

In the 18th and 19th centuries, organised Alpine expeditions helped develop climbing equipment, route planning and mountain guiding. Over time, climbers explored the Himalayas and other major ranges.

Modern mountaineering combines traditional knowledge with improved weather forecasting, communication and safety practices.



Important milestones

Early exploration

Mountain travel supported trade, science, culture and local communities long before climbing became a sport.

Alpine era

Organised climbing in the Alps shaped guiding, route descriptions and climbing traditions.

Modern expeditions

Today, teams use technical training, forecasts and careful logistics to reduce risk.

Remember: Mountain history includes the knowledge and contributions of local guides and communities, whose expertise remains essential to safe and respectful expeditions.
