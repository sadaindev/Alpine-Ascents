Sheltering in the Mountains

Rest, warmth and protection are key parts of expedition planning.

Mountain Tents

Choose a shelter designed for expected wind, precipitation and temperature. Select a safe site away from exposed ridges and hazards.

Established Campsites

Use permitted sites, follow local rules, store food responsibly and minimise impact on fragile vegetation.

Staying Warm

Keep dry layers available, protect yourself from wind and plan sleeping systems for the forecast conditions.

Leave no trace: Pack out rubbish, respect wildlife, avoid damaging plants and follow local guidance on fires and sanitation.
