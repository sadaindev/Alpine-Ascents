Mountaineering Records

Notable milestones from the world’s highest mountains.

Notable achievements

Records can change as new expeditions take place. The examples below provide context and are not a live ranking.

Highest mountain


Mount Everest

Earth’s highest mountain above sea level, in the Himalayas on the Nepal–China border. Elevation is commonly given as 8,848.86 metres.

Karakoram


K2

The world’s second-highest mountain, at about 8,611 metres, located in the Karakoram range. It is known for demanding climbing conditions.

Pakistan


Mountain Heritage

Pakistan is home to several major peaks in the Karakoram, Himalaya and Hindu Kush, drawing climbers and trekkers from around the world.

For official current records, consult recognised mountaineering organisations and reliable expedition reports. Routes, conditions and record claims should be verified before publication.
